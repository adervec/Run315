What this number is not

It is not comparable with anyone else’s. Fast runners race at around 96% of critical speed from the gun and have no room to drift, so their fade shows up as slowing rather than as a climbing heart rate. Men decouple more than women, and earlier. The bands here come from a study of 82,303 recreational marathon runners, and the thresholds in it are described by its own authors as arbitrary.

It is also not a health measurement, and a single run says little. What is worth reading is the direction across a block of similar long runs.
