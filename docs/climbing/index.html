Against the energy model

You hold 14% more speed on the climbs than the energy model expects. Hills are a strength.

Measured inside each run: your speed on the flat sets the reference, and every climbing segment is compared against the speed Minetti's energy-cost model says the same effort should buy on that gradient. Because both halves come from the same run, fitness, weather and how hard you were trying all cancel — only the gradient is left.

This is the part grade-adjusted pace cannot tell you. GAP applies the model to flatten your splits; this tests it, which is why it can say whether hills are a strength.
