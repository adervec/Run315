Testing 108 correlations at the usual 5% threshold would throw up about 5 false positives from noise alone — and in fact 36 of yours would have cleared that bar. Everything above is corrected for the number of tests, so the threshold is r > 0.44 instead.

Two honest caveats. Hard days cluster and bad nights cluster, so your days are not independent and the real evidence is weaker than the arithmetic suggests. And correlation is not causation: a daily record cannot tell you which way the arrow points. Read these as dials worth glancing at, not as findings.

Left out as arithmetic rather than insight: intensity minutes +0.95active calories +0.94total calories +0.91daily steps +0.90vigorous minutes +0.86 — calories and step counts rise on days you run further because that is what they measure.

How your nights are built

74 nights · 6.6 h asleep on average

Deep 21% Light 58% REM 21%

Your nights are built in roughly the usual adult proportions. Sleep staging from a wrist device is an estimate, so read this as a shape rather than a measurement.

Adults typically run 13–23% deep and 20–25% REM. Yours are both inside those ranges.
